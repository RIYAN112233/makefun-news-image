# The World on One Page — Saturday 21 November 2026

An entry for the make.fun bounty
[news-image-20261121](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261121):
sum up the world news of Saturday 21 November 2026 in one image.

The image is a newspaper front page: a masthead naming the day, and six
panels, one per story, each a flat editorial illustration with a short
factual caption. All lettering was typeset programmatically, so nothing in
the page is model-drawn text. `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only)
copies the finished `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`; it runs offline and
writes identical bytes on every run.

## The six stories, and where they were reported

1. **Conflict — Kryvyi Rih, Ukraine.** A Russian ballistic missile struck
   the centre of Kryvyi Rih, President Zelensky's hometown, killing two
   people and wounding 60; Russian missiles and a drone also came down in
   Poland, which sought more NATO air defence. (One Turn daily digest,
   21 Nov 2026, reporting CBC/DW/Straits Times; WW6RAG Daily Brief,
   21 Nov 2026.)
2. **Economy — world markets.** An AI stock sell-off spread across world
   markets: the S&P 500 fell 3.7% to 6,910 and the Nasdaq fell 5.3%,
   giving up its 2026 gain, as Nvidia dropped about 14% on a report that
   the SEC is examining its accounting. (One Turn; WW6RAG, both
   21 Nov 2026.)
3. **Justice — The Hague.** The International Court of Justice found
   Myanmar responsible for genocide against the Rohingya. (One Turn,
   21 Nov 2026.)
4. **Crime — Guasave, Sinaloa, Mexico.** Mexican forces captured Fausto
   Isidro Meza Flores, "El Chapo Isidro", an FBI most-wanted cartel boss;
   violence followed in Sinaloa. (One Turn; PBS NewsHour and NPR headlines,
   21 Nov 2026.)
5. **Climate — Antalya, Türkiye.** The COP31 climate summit overran its
   Friday deadline, deadlocked over fossil-fuel language in the draft
   text. (One Turn; WW6RAG, 21 Nov 2026.)
6. **Space — Mercury.** ESA and JAXA's BepiColombo spacecraft reached
   Mercury and entered orbit after an eight-year journey. (One Turn;
   Eos, "BepiColombo Arrives at Mercury", 21 Nov 2026.)
